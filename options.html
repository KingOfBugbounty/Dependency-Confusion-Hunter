ℹ️ How it works

The extension passively monitors JavaScript files and source maps loaded by the pages you visit. It extracts package names and checks if they exist in npm, pip, RubyGems, crates.io, NuGet e Maven Central. Packages that do not exist publicly can be targets for Dependency Confusion attacks.
